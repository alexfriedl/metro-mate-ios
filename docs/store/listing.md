## Description

Metro Mate is a metronome for drummers. Calm between the beats, clear on the beat. Free, open source, no ads, no subscription, no account.

THE CLICK
- A wave field of connected dots flows in tempo and jumps on every beat, accents hit harder
- Tempo wheel from 1 to 300 BPM with a haptic tick per BPM and a stronger one every 10
- Beat grid with accents and note values from quarters to sixteenth triplets
- Tap tempo, shuffle a pattern, save your own presets
- 7 click sounds: Classic woodblock, Clean, Claves, Pocket, Cowbell, Punch, Deep
- Keeps playing with the ringer on silent and in the background

YOUR SOUND
- Build your own drum kit for Learn: 8 snares, 6 kicks, 5 hi-hats, 4 open hi-hats and 3 practice pad sounds, each with a preview
- Import your own one-shots from the Files app, for example an export from your DAW. They stay on your device

LEARN
- 70 grooves and exercises in 9 categories, Beginner to Advanced
- All-Time Classics in the style of Funky Drummer, Purdie Shuffle, Amen Break and more
- Funk and ghost notes, linear grooves, hi-hat work, kick independence, shuffles and triplets, accent studies, chops with R and L sticking
- Every groove as a grid you can hear, played on a recorded funk kit
- Dynamics on or off, accents and ghost notes or every hit at one level
- Touch the grid to hear one column at a time, loop the part you are working on
- Remembers your tempo for every groove, one tap back to the target tempo

HISTORY
- Week view with practice time per day
- Month view with calendar, longest streak and tempo progress per groove
- Stored on your device only

Siri: "Start Metro Mate at 90 BPM".

The drum and click samples are recorded instruments released under CC0, the Clean tone is generated in the app. The code is on GitHub, so the no tracking promise is something you can check.
