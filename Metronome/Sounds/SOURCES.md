# Sound sources

All samples in this folder are public domain (CC0 1.0). They were trimmed, faded and normalized for Metro Mate, the snare was mixed from its top, bottom and overhead microphones and tuned up slightly. The Clean tone is synthesized in code.

- Snare (`snare*.wav`): Frankensnare by Karoryfer Samples, 14x8 aluminum snare, accent is the rimshot. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.frankensnare
- Practice pad (`pad-*.wav`): "Perc_PracticePad_lo" by LudwigMueller on Freesound, CC0. https://freesound.org/people/LudwigMueller/sounds/548525/ (brightened, accents with a sharper attack, the left hand tuned down slightly)
- Kick and hi-hat (`kick*.wav`, `hihat*.wav`, kick EQed and shortened for a tight punchy attack): Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
- Woodblock, claves, cowbell, shaker: Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios. https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
