# Sound sources

All samples in this folder come from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).

- Library: https://versilian-studios.com/vcsl/
- Repository: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

They were trimmed, faded and peak-normalized for Metro Mate. The Clean tone is synthesized in code.
